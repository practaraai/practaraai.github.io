Privacy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or trackers.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

What we collect

When you request an audit we collect your practice name, contact name, work email, phone, website address, practice city and state, practice type, preferred call time, any message you write, and a referral code or page name if you arrived from a link. When you use the instant score we save the website address you enter and its score, grade, load time, mobile speed score, and the top issue found. If you request a deeper analysis from your scorecard, that score and grade are saved with your request, along with a note that it came from the scorecard.

How it is processed

This information is sent to a Google Apps Script web app and stored in Practara AI's Google Sheet, so Google processes it through Google Apps Script and Google Sheets, including your IP address, under Google's terms. To score a website, our server fetches that site's public homepage, robots.txt, and sitemap, and may send the address to Google PageSpeed Insights.

How we use it

We use this information only to prepare your audit and to contact you about it. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep it for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner. Email practara.ai@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete your information.

Patient information

These forms are for practice owners and staff only. Please do not send patient information. If you are a patient, please contact your doctor's office directly.

Questions

Contact practara.ai@gmail.com.
