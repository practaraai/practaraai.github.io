What this site collects

This website has no analytics, ad pixels, cookies, or third-party trackers. It does not collect information on its own.

Audit requests

When you request an audit, your email app sends us the details you choose to include, such as your name, practice, website, email, and phone. We use them only to prepare your audit and contact you about it. We do not sell this information.

Patient information

Please do not send patient information through this site or by email. Practara AI does not need patient information to audit a website. If a client engagement ever requires access to systems that could hold protected health information, we sign a Business Associate Agreement first.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy statement.
