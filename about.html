About Practara AI

Why we exist

Many physicians are excellent. They use advanced technology, modern procedures, and refined techniques, and they have serious training. Their websites show almost none of it. Patients cannot see what makes the practice good, so they book somewhere that looks more current.

Most practices do not know what they do not know about marketing: who their right patient is, where those patients go instead, and why they pick a competitor. We find the gap between how good the practice really is and what its website and AI answers say, then close it.
