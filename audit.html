Free website audit

Find out what patients, and AI search, can and cannot see about your practice.

What we check

  • Modern website: mobile layout, speed, HTTPS, broken links, stale content, page titles and descriptions
  • AI visibility: AI crawler access, llms.txt, medical schema, FAQ content
  • Listings: name, address, and phone consistency, Google Business Profile, Healthgrades, Vitals, NPPES
  • Technology and procedure gap: what you do compared with what your site shows, cited to public sources
  • Where your patients go: who ranks and gets named by AI tools for your key procedures, and why
  • Compliance risk flags: ad pixels on patient pages, legacy tracking, forms that may collect patient information, accessibility basics (informational, not legal advice)
